# Creative direction

Use this when the user needs a script or expressive approach from a content goal. With a complete prompt, script, audio, or processing request, go directly to the relevant capability. For a focused revision, start with [review](../capabilities/analysis/video-review.md). Creative judgment belongs to the host, not a new API.

Establish only what changes the current decision: audience/viewing context; desired understanding or action; real facts/evidence; supplied media and hard constraints; and an appropriate form such as demonstration, narration, conversation, graphics, or a combination. Do not impose a purchase CTA, effects, extra shots, or a planning form.

Produce enough direction to continue into [talking heads](../workflows/oral-broadcast.md), [short drama](../workflows/short-drama.md), [reference adaptation](../workflows/reference-remake.md), or another [workflow](../catalog.md). Reuse upstream planning rather than repeat it. Authorized creation permits ordinary creative choices; a planning-only request ends with planning. Check downstream feasibility before generating paid intermediates.

Ground prices, offers, efficacy, reviews, and endorsements in actual evidence. Source marketing claims retain that status. Ask for consequential missing facts or choose an approach that does not require them; generated pictures are not proof.

## Coordinate content, picture, and sound

Let the opening create an expectation that the rest fulfills. Show a product immediately when that serves the work; a story may establish its situation first. Avoid universal reveal delays or fixed hook formulas.

Give each needed shot a purpose: establish, demonstrate, substantiate, respond, or conclude. Write observable actions, gaze, object states, and changes. One sufficient shot need not become many.

Decide whether speech, picture, or both carry the information. B-roll should explain or show evidence, not illustrate every noun mechanically. Plan for silent viewing when it is actually relevant.

Keep one adopted spoken script. Verbatim captions and independent headlines have different jobs. Check real-language reading time and avoid obscuring important text, products, faces, or actions. Exact data/branding may need [controlled postproduction](../workflows/hybrid-video.md). Planned seconds are targets, not verified output timing.

Track stable character, clothing, scene, product shape, and markings when continuity matters, alongside intentional changes. Assign each real reference a role: identity, product, space, composition, motion, or sound. Inspect a previous generation before using it as a new reference so mistakes do not propagate. Use only reference types supported by the selected model and public fields.

Use [Meta methods](../platforms/meta/index.md) only for the relevant platform task. Exploration counts, 5V, geographic styling, placement defaults, and model-specific prompting are conditional methods. Preserve current decisions and resumable work in [creative handoff](../contracts/creative-handoff.md).
