# Product marketing video

Create product demonstrations, lifestyle videos, digital-presenter videos or multi-shot ecommerce ads from a product link, saved product or customer facts; continue revisions too. A link with “make an ad for US customers” connects product retrieval, saving, references, creative direction and actual video production.

## Prepare the product and concept

Use [product inputs](../resources/products.md) to parse/select, reuse an identical saved product or save a new one, then read detail and references. Link-based production includes saving by default. Respect analysis-only, planning-only and no-save requests. Reuse existing products and complete facts without mandatory reimport, parsing or competitor research.

Retain market, audience, placement/ratio, language, duration, style, claims and count. Use English for US customers when unspecified, respecting explicit alternatives. Do not invent a dollar price without a real US price. Keep facts, AI profiles and creative judgments separate, carrying forbidden expressions into copy, picture, speech and captions.

Use [creative direction](../creation/creative-direction.md) when needed. Choose a clear message supported by real features or use. Design opening, demonstration, evidence and ending for this task rather than imposing one ad structure. Show portability through actual storage/carrying without invented weights or comparisons. Preserve supplied scripts/prompts/demonstrations. Explicit production requests authorize routine creative decisions without mandatory script/storyboard approval stages.

## Production routes

| Need | Route |
| --- | --- |
| One demonstration/ad with adequate references | [Video generation](../capabilities/generation/video-generation.md), passing real product images through supported reference/first-frame inputs |
| New lifestyle/product images or keyframes | [Marketing images](product-marketing-image.md), inspect and adopt only needed images before generating their shots; do not automatically create a full set |
| Presenter-led content | [Talking heads](oral-broadcast.md), adding product shots as needed without invented real customer endorsements |
| Multiple separately generated shots | [Storyboard production](storyboard-video.md), fixing variant, reference and voice relationships before assembly |
| Exact text, charts, animation or complete templates | [Programmatic video](../capabilities/generation/programmatic-video.md); use [B-roll](broll-production.md) for an insert |
| Precise joining/packaging of existing shots | [Hybrid production](hybrid-video.md); explicit Vibbit captions/packaging use the relevant cloud route |

Check downstream voice, composition and input dependencies before generating paid media. Pass real image references; naming the product alone does not constrain appearance. Inspect intermediate images for model, color, proportions, logos and packaging before adopting them for shots.

Split production according to model limits when necessary, without copying reference timestamps. Adopt narration before generating voice and base captions on the actual adopted audio. AI copy is not automatically a confirmed product fact. One video does not authorize variants; keep explicit goals and counts for sets/variants.

When speech needs checking, [transcribe](../capabilities/analysis/speech-transcription.md) adopted audio once and use [difference reports](../runtime/transcript-tools.md) for brands, models, prices, units, and adopted speech, then listen to uncertainties. Captions and sentence-based product shots share sentence times; word-triggered benefit cards require complete valid word evidence. Card-style changes do not restart speech or ASR.

## Review, deliver and continue

Submit through the chosen capabilities and retain task IDs. Product preparation, shots, composition and effect review are separate states. Replace the [example request](../../examples/product-marketing-video.json) references and prompt with the chosen product and current brief.

Use [video review](../capabilities/analysis/video-review.md): consistent product/variant appearance, real usage, supported claims, audience/language fit, correct text/audio, and unobscured demonstrations. Claim, shot, background and language changes affect only relevant dependencies; retain the product and usable assets instead of rerunning everything.

Deliver the actual video with essential parameters, product ID, source, adopted references, copy and shot/task relationships. Saving product facts does not attach generated media, create editable projects or save platform versions; current product commands do not perform those actions. Deliver images and videos separately for combined requests, explaining their relationship.
