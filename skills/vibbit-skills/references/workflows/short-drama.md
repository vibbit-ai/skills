# Short drama

Move a story, script, or existing project toward the requested script, storyboard, shots, or final video. Continue from adopted work; a clearly described single shot can use [video generation](../capabilities/generation/video-generation.md) directly.

A premise needs character objectives, conflict, and this episode's change. An adopted script needs only missing shot design. A complete board goes to [storyboard production](storyboard-video.md). Existing shots need verification and selection, then only missing/unsuitable parts. A sequel begins from adopted characters/world, previous ending, and unresolved events.

Check references, shot generation, sound, and assembly capabilities before producing a batch. Map dialogue and voice to each role; [audio generation](../capabilities/generation/audio-generation.md) can prepare speech, music, and effects as needed. If a required voice or postproduction route is unavailable, state the concrete gap before paying for dependent assets. Local scripts/media are not automatically saved editable platform drama projects.

## Story through action

Give key characters an objective, obstacle, and choice. Show conflict through behavior and dialogue rather than announcing tension in narration. Establish what changes in knowledge, possession, situation, or relationship during each scene; remove shots without a narrative purpose.

Dialogue acts on another character: testing, refusing, concealing, persuading. Allow reactions and comprehension time. Distinguish voiceover from visible dialogue, and match speaker, sound, and mouth visibility.

Resolve the episode's main change. A series can retain grounded open questions; every story need not end in a twist. Preserve user plot and adopted words, identifying specific production or continuity conflicts when changes are needed.

## Continuity

Track real reference assets separately from character descriptions:
- Characters: appearance, clothing, voice, relationship, current motive.
- Scenes: layout, time/light, relative positions, gaze, and screen direction.
- Props: ownership, handling, opened/broken/transferred state, and reasons for changes.
- Episodes: prior endpoint, known information, changed relationships, unresolved events.

A new character description does not create an avatar resource or guarantee identity locking. Inspect shared character/space references before using them in dependent shots. Do not inherit an erroneous previous-shot state automatically.

## Shots, sound, and assembly

Use enough shot detail for production: narrative change, characters/action, framing/gaze/camera needs, speech/reactions/sound, and asset dependencies. Keep stable shot IDs for revision. Planned duration is a target; actual voice, performance, and editing determine rhythm.

Write motivation and visible response. Dialogue can use listener reactions and offscreen speech; avoid mechanical one-line/one-cut patterns or converting every drama into separate avatar monologues.

Hand off to [storyboard production](storyboard-video.md) and the selected model guide. A short representative test can validate key continuity/performance when useful; do not mandate extra candidates or approval at every stage.

Assemble through [hybrid production](hybrid-video.md), deliberately selecting original/generated/replaced tracks and avoiding duplicate voices. Inspect actual speech, voice continuity, and lip sync. Speech-driven graphics/sound use [semantic timing](../creation/script-and-timing.md); silent actions can use a scene clock. Missing word evidence remains pending. Repeated work can use [production plans](../contracts/production-plan.md).

For dialogue checks or captions, reuse each adopted audio version's [transcript and difference report](../runtime/transcript-tools.md). Compare role lines and listen to omissions, repeats, and names; actual speech determines caption placement. Roles come from the script, voice assets, and performance, not ASR channels/emotion. A line replacement updates its voice, lip sync, and timing while retaining unaffected shots.

## Continue and review

Recover current script/shot revisions, selections, real tasks, and issues. Preserve shot identity across reordering.

A bad action affects that shot and neighboring action/prop/gaze/sound continuity. A line change affects speech/captions/lip sync/duration and any changed story information. A new appearance/voice affects shots using it; distinguish a one-scene costume change from a revised permanent design. Pace changes first examine cuts/reactions/order before regenerating. A new ending/sequel retains still-valid world and character material.

Generative edits have their actual limits and cannot promise frame locks. Deliver revised media and local editable records without claiming unsupported platform writeback.

[Review](../capabilities/analysis/video-review.md) motivation, reaction, causality, reveals, spatial/prop continuity, speaker/voice/lip/caption correspondence, and the relation to prior episodes. Label scripts, shot assets, and final assembled videos accurately and preserve editable source when requested.
