# Product research and strategy

Turn product leads, content observations, customer feedback, and supplier information into explainable priorities and a validation plan. Use this for opportunity discovery, single-product assessment, candidate comparison, and image/video-based product or supplier discovery.

Use only sources needed for the question. A simple video/review/supplier lookup can go straight to its resource. Strategy-only discussion may use existing material with explicit unknowns; a research plan is not a completed market finding.

## Scope and entry point

Retain target market, sales channel, audience/category, price/cost constraints, exclusions, and requested deliverable. Ask only for gaps that change search or ranking. Without a market, explore directions without presenting local-market conclusions.

Choose an entry:
- Opportunity discovery: search product/use-case video leads, then investigate specific candidates.
- Need-led research: use discussions about difficulties and alternatives, then examine positive/negative product reviews.
- Image lookup: identify the real target product, find similar listings, verify variant/specifications, then search suppliers.
- Candidate comparison: use the same market, purpose, and evidence scope for all selected products.

If the user already chose a product, assess its claims, weaknesses, or sourcing instead of selecting a different one.

## Source roles and dependencies

| Source | Input and purpose | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Google/TikTok videos](../resources/external/videos.md) | Queries, region/language where supported; candidates, demonstrations, content approaches | Rank/frequency/engagement do not establish sales or an upward trend |
| [Visual matches](../resources/external/visual-matches.md) | Real accessible product image; identify related models/pages | Similar appearance does not establish identical product/manufacturer |
| [1688 suppliers](../resources/external/suppliers.md) | Real accessible product image; sourcing/specification leads | Not keyword search; cite prices/MOQ only when actually returned |
| [Amazon reviews](../resources/external/reviews.md) | Confirmed real product-page URL; reasons to buy and actual experiences | Not product search; do not invent an ASIN or URL |
| [Reddit discussions](../resources/external/reviews.md) | Relevant query; contexts, concerns, alternatives | Participants are not necessarily buyers; query wording does not establish their location |

Preserve TikTok's actual `sortType`/`publishTime` fields and documented meanings. Reddit has no dedicated region/language field. A visual match may not yield an Amazon page; acquire and verify one before requesting its reviews. If a real product screenshot is needed, inspect the actual video and use [media inputs](../runtime/media-inputs.md) to provide an accessible image. A generated concept image is not evidence for sourcing the original product.

## Build an evidence-based decision

Start with a small useful exploration, such as five results per necessary query, then fill consequential gaps. Respect scope and budget rather than querying all sources for every candidate.

Keep stable candidate IDs, actual names/variants, images, and links. Separate lookalikes, specifications, bundles, and single items. Deduplicate reposts and repeated pages. Do not combine another variant's reviews and price into the target's attributes.

Observe actual demonstration, context, dates, and returned engagement. Search snippets support only snippet-level conclusions; watch/analyze when visual details matter. Trend claims need comparable observations across time.

Connect feedback → possible improvement → condition to validate. Distinguish product quality, usage, shipping, and support issues. A complaint about cleaning may suggest a removable design, but does not prove supply exists or customers will pay more. Preserve counterevidence.

For findings, retain source, query/image/product URL, region/language filters, retrieval time, available publication date, and actual sample size. Page review totals differ from retrieved sample counts. Only report M of N when counted; do not extrapolate to market share. Ads, duplicates, and hearsay are not independent demand evidence.

Compare supplier model, features, material, size, and packaging. Record actual currency, quantity tier, variant, time, and fee coverage. Missing supplier/MOQ/sample/delivery data remains unknown; the lowest displayed price is not automatically the target procurement price.

Profit scenarios need actual sale price, procurement, shipping, channel fees, taxes, returns, and acquisition costs where relevant. Keep unknowns and scenarios explicit rather than assigning zero and declaring profit. User constraints take precedence over a composite score.

## Priorities and next validation

Compare demand evidence, meaningful differentiation, demonstrable content value, and supply/economics. Label support, counterevidence, and unknowns. Missing data is not proof of poor performance.

Recommend “prioritize validation,” “gather evidence,” or “do not pursue,” with reasons. Avoid invented viral probabilities or precise scores. If scoring is requested, state weights, common criteria, evidence, and coverage; scores support this comparison, not market prediction.

For a priority candidate, name the audience/context, position, features to retain/improve, content-test angle, sourcing checks, largest uncertainty, and smallest decision-changing next test. Views/clicks alone do not validate purchase conversion. Define stop/continue conditions from real goals and costs, not a universal viral threshold.

Deliver scope, candidate findings/sources/gaps, and the next strategy in a proportionate report or comparison table. Preserve partial results when a source fails. An empty or unfamiliar response is unavailable interpretable evidence, not zero demand/reviews/suppliers. Resume returned task IDs, and stop expanding searches when results repeat or cannot resolve the important gap.

Continuation retains candidate identity, evidence, and adopted decisions, refreshing only relevant stale/missing information. Research alone delivers findings. When the user explicitly requests saving selected candidates, use [product library management](../resources/products.md), verify identity, and report saving only after receiving real IDs. Do not claim unperformed supplier contact, purchasing, or ad launches. Continue to [marketing images](product-marketing-image.md), [marketing videos](product-video.md), or another creation workflow only when requested.
