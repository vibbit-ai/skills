# Target Audience Motives

Read [shared boundaries](guidelines.md). Use this to explore audiences, buying reasons, or creative bottlenecks; skip it when the selected audience/motive already suffices.

Collect actual product benefits, brand/category, market, existing audience/creative, known bottleneck, and intended action. Missing campaign data permits hypotheses but cannot prove an audience is uncovered.

Start from practical value, emotional benefit, and real contexts rather than widening age/gender mechanically. Describe observable scenes, behaviors, mindset, and relevant demographics, with a clear product connection. Without account evidence, label proposed groups as new-audience hypotheses.

Write each motive as a consumer's inner sentence with a short explanation, such as “When I get home, I do not want another half hour of tidying.” Avoid using abstract psychological categories as ad copy or rephrasing one motive ten times. Distinguish pre-purchase concern, use moment, desired outcome, and social influence.

Reuse existing research. For current facts, use browsing or [product research](../../workflows/product-research.md), retaining source, market, time, and sample. Reviews, surveys, and account data have different evidential scope. Never invent percentages, reports, links, or ad results.

Full exploration uses three audiences × ten motives; user counts and local revisions override that recipe. Each audience has `audience_id`, name, concise profile, rationale, and evidence/hypothesis status. Each motive has `motive_id`, inner monologue, meaning, and product connection.

Use grouped tables and [handoff](../../contracts/creative-handoff.md). Pass only adopted audiences/motives to [hooks](emotional-hooks.md) or [localization](localization.md); unselected ideas do not automatically become paid media.
