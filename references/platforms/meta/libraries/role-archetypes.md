# Twelve brand archetypes

Use only when choosing a new [Role Play](../role-play.md) character. Content jobs are tendencies; one archetype can serve different jobs, while a complete three-role set uses distinct archetypes. Consider product fit, then market, then a concrete character.

| Archetype | Drive | Emotional appeal | Typical job | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Innocent | Safety, optimism, simplicity | Hope, trust, nostalgia | Social momentum | Warm, honest, uncomplicated |
| Sage | Understanding, truth, knowledge | Clarity, confidence | Rational proof | Analytical, restrained, educational |
| Explorer | Freedom, discovery, adventure | Curiosity, independence, wonder | Emotional pull | Bold, authentic, experiential |
| Ruler | Control, mastery, high standards | Status, order, exclusivity | Rational proof | Refined, decisive, organized |
| Creator | Innovation, expression, craft | Inspiration, originality | Emotional pull | Imaginative, detail-oriented |
| Caregiver | Nurturing, protection, service | Comfort, trust, care | Social momentum | Gentle, empathetic, patient |
| Magician | Transformation, possibility | Awe, wonder | Emotional pull | Charismatic, visionary, dramatic |
| Hero | Mastery, courage, achievement | Empowerment, victory | Social momentum | Disciplined, ambitious, active |
| Rebel | Disruption, liberation | Defiance, rule-breaking | Emotional pull | Provocative, uncompromising |
| Jester | Joy, humor, the present | Fun, connection through laughter | Emotional pull | Witty, playful, self-deprecating |
| Lover | Intimacy, sensory pleasure | Desire, beauty, belonging | Social momentum | Passionate, sensory, aesthetic |
| Everyman | Belonging, authenticity | Connection, recognition, trust | Social momentum | Casual, honest, grounded |

Explain how the role contributes credibility, connection, or information. An archetype is not an avatar resource or proof of professional credentials. Reusing an already selected role does not require rereading all archetypes.
