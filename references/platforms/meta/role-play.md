# Creator roles and brand archetypes

Use the contents to read only the section needed for the current operation. Each section retains its own parameters, authorization, and execution boundaries.

- [Role Play](#role-play)

<a id="role-play"></a>

## Role Play

Read [shared boundaries](guidelines.md). This module designs creator identity, visual continuity, and expression. It does not clone a real person, create an avatar, or produce a callable resource ID. Existing audio plus an avatar goes straight to [talking heads](../../workflows/oral-broadcast.md).

Preserve selected appearance/voice and use actual product benefits, tone, audience/market, content task, and references. For a new role, consult [12 archetypes](#role-archetypes). A full three-role set uses distinct archetypes covering:
- `emotional_pull`: Jester, Explorer, Rebel, Magician, Creator.
- `rational_proof`: Sage, Ruler, Creator, Caregiver.
- `social_momentum`: Everyman, Hero, Lover, Innocent, Caregiver.

Evaluate product/category fit, then actual market relevance. A one-role request does not need the other two. Turn an abstract archetype into a concrete memorable creator, explaining the information, credibility, or connection it offers. Age/ethnicity are not substitutes for evidence or shortcuts to cultural stereotypes.

<a id="role-play-character-sheet"></a>

### Character sheet

Include role ID, base archetype, role name, tagline, content job, value function, and rationale; relevant appearance and optional genuinely needed ethnicity note; default wardrobe with logic/exclusions; environment with logic/exclusions; expression/energy/gesture defaults; 2–3 concrete continuity rules as a starting point; and messaging habits, exact preserved words, prohibited claims, and brand boundaries.

A role-led ad may start at 15–25 seconds and 4–5 cards, subject to the request. Use [VB-04](storyboarder.md#storyboarder) for production-card handoff.

Use a real existing avatar where appropriate. Its public video input is `audio_url + digital_human_id`, not wardrobe/scene/camera control. New clothing/scenes may use supported image/video generation, but a character picture is not a new avatar resource. Explicit cloning follows [creation boundaries](../../capabilities/generation/digital-human-creation.md).

Reuse original local/remote audio. For text-only input, generate and inspect actual speech, then use the returned URL. Keep creative role IDs distinct from server IDs in [handoff](../../contracts/creative-handoff.md), and label role sheets, prompts, and real media by their actual state.

## Twelve brand archetypes

Use only when choosing a new [Role Play](#role-play) character. Content jobs are tendencies; one archetype can serve different jobs, while a complete three-role set uses distinct archetypes. Consider product fit, then market, then a concrete character.

| Archetype | Drive | Emotional appeal | Typical job | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Innocent | Safety, optimism, simplicity | Hope, trust, nostalgia | Social momentum | Warm, honest, uncomplicated |
| Sage | Understanding, truth, knowledge | Clarity, confidence | Rational proof | Analytical, restrained, educational |
| Explorer | Freedom, discovery, adventure | Curiosity, independence, wonder | Emotional pull | Bold, authentic, experiential |
| Ruler | Control, mastery, high standards | Status, order, exclusivity | Rational proof | Refined, decisive, organized |
| Creator | Innovation, expression, craft | Inspiration, originality | Emotional pull | Imaginative, detail-oriented |
| Caregiver | Nurturing, protection, service | Comfort, trust, care | Social momentum | Gentle, empathetic, patient |
| Magician | Transformation, possibility | Awe, wonder | Emotional pull | Charismatic, visionary, dramatic |
| Hero | Mastery, courage, achievement | Empowerment, victory | Social momentum | Disciplined, ambitious, active |
| Rebel | Disruption, liberation | Defiance, rule-breaking | Emotional pull | Provocative, uncompromising |
| Jester | Joy, humor, the present | Fun, connection through laughter | Emotional pull | Witty, playful, self-deprecating |
| Lover | Intimacy, sensory pleasure | Desire, beauty, belonging | Social momentum | Passionate, sensory, aesthetic |
| Everyman | Belonging, authenticity | Connection, recognition, trust | Social momentum | Casual, honest, grounded |

Explain how the role contributes credibility, connection, or information. An archetype is not an avatar resource or proof of professional credentials. Reusing an already selected role does not require rereading all archetypes.
